# Data license

The data we created (the item sets in `data/materials/`, the results ledger, the figure data in
`docs/paper-prep/figdata/` and the analysis plan in `docs/analysis-plan/`) are released under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Please cite the paper
when you use them.

Third-party material keeps its own terms:

- `data/neutral_text/`: passages from WikiText-103 (Merity et al., 2017), CC BY-SA 3.0.
- M3 items: definitions drafted from WordNet 3.0 glosses (Princeton University), WordNet 3.0 license.
- Homograph families: dominance norms of Gilbert and Rodd (2022).
- `data/literature/`: values transcribed from the publications cited in each row.
- Not included and fetched instead: Wu and Hoffman (2022) item properties, SWOW-EN18, the USF free association norms
  and SUBTLEX-US (see `data/raw/SOURCES.md`).
